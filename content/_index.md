---
title: No Cap Workshop
headline: We compete on features. We collaborate on security.
eyebrow: A Critical Sec initiative · January 26–28, 2027 · Free and online
lede: No Cap Workshop is three days of free, practical, practitioner-led sessions on meeting FedRAMP's VDR rules before the December 7, 2026 deadline. No sales pitches and no low-effort webinars. Practitioners share the strategies and tactics they are using right now, because the government we are all protecting is the same one.

name_note: >-
  **Why “No Cap”?** CAP is a corrective action plan, the status a provider lands in if it misses the
  December 7 deadline. Offerings under a CAP keep their certification only through March 7, 2027.
  This workshop is about staying out from under one, or getting out.

how_heading: Free, open, and anonymous.
how: >-
  This is a free online event open to anyone who wants to take part. There is no pre-registration
  and no identification, between you and us or otherwise. Show up, watch, ask questions, and
  participate at whatever level of engagement works for you. Every session is recorded and posted
  online for everyone to use afterward.

days_heading: Three days, one pipeline.
days_note: Session times are shown in your local time.

attend_heading: Who should attend
attend:
  - title: Teams under a corrective action plan
    text: Find the strategies and approaches that get you out from under it by March 7, 2027.
  - title: Teams not under a CAP
    text: Find new ways to strengthen what you already do, and borrow from the experience of others.
  - title: Teams in a good place
    text: Show up to share what is working, and probably learn something along the way.

speakers_heading: Who speaks
speakers_text: >-
  Speakers are active practitioners from cloud service providers, auditors, advisors, and vendors.
  We organize tracks and sessions around the work, not around anyone's product.

measure_heading: How we measure success
measure: >-
  By how many teams come off a CAP, or avoid going on one, and are positioned to succeed by the
  March 7 deadline and beyond. The bias is toward immediate, actionable, real-world strategies that
  people have already put in place.

sponsors_heading: Sponsors
sponsors_text: >-
  The workshop is free to attend because sponsors make it so. Sponsors fund practitioner time,
  recording, and publication, and share the room with a security-minded audience that is working
  on this exact problem.

closing_heading: This is bigger than the rules.
closing: >-
  The point is not just to meet the VDR rules. It is to drive effective solutions and decisions
  that reduce the vulnerability of our services, and therefore of our government.
---
