title: No Cap Workshop
headline: We compete on features. We collaborate on security.
eyebrow: Nation-Wide Workshop · January 26–28, 2027
lede: The No Cap Workshop is three days of free, practical, practitioner-led sessions to support teams working on compliance with FedRAMP's VDR requirements. No sales pitches and no low-effort lectures. The people who have successfully met these challenges will share the strategies and tactics they are using right now, because at the end of the day it's our government that we're all protecting.

name_note: >-
  **Why “No Cap”?** <br/>A CAP is a Corrective Action Plan, the status a Cloud Service Provider lands in if it misses the
  [December 7 deadline](https://www.fedramp.gov/notices/0014/) for implementing FedRAMP's [CR-26](https://fedramp.gov/2026/) rules for Vulnerability Detection and Response  ([VDR](https://fedramp.gov/2026/agencies/rules/vulnerability-evaluation-and-reporting/)). Offerings under a CAP are at risk of having their FedRAMP certification revoked if they are not compliant by March 7, 2027. <br/><br/>
  This workshop is about staying out of a CAP, or getting out if you're already in.

workshop_heading: Nation-Wide Workshop
workshop: >-
  One workshop, with leaders drawn from around the country. For three days, practitioners who have
  done this work join online to meet teams where they are: working through VDR alongside you instead
  of lecturing at you. Each day takes one stage of the work, from data collection to VDR Evaluation
  to remediation and reporting, and multi-day tracks follow a single approach from start to finish so
  you can see the whole path and not just the pieces.

how_heading: Free, open, and accessible.
how: >-
  This is a free online event open to anyone who wants to take part. There is no registration
  and no identification. Show up, watch, learn, ask questions, and
  participate at whatever level of engagement works for you. Each session will be recorded and posted
  online for everyone to use afterward.

days_heading: Three Days, Strategic Deliverables.
days_note: Session times are shown in your local time.

attend_heading: Who Should Attend
attend:
  - title: Teams under a CAP
    text: Learn the strategies and approaches that get you out from under it by March 7, 2027.
  - title: Teams not under a CAP
    text: Find new ways to strengthen what you already do, and borrow from the experience of others.
  - title: Teams in a Good Place
    text: Show up to share what is working, and learn a few new tricks along the way.

leads_heading: Who Leads
leads_text: >-
  Leads are active practitioners from Cloud Service Providers, auditors, advisors, and vendors.
  We organize tracks and sessions around the work. If your product has answers, this is a forum to let everyone know.

measure_heading: The Mission
measure: >-
  Support teams to come off a CAP, or avoid going on one, then position everyone to succeed by the
  March 7 deadline and beyond.

sponsors_heading: Sponsors
sponsors_text: >-
  The workshop is free to attend because sponsors make it happen. Sponsors fund logistics, time,
  and publication, and share the room with a security-minded audience that is working
  on this exact problem.

closing_heading: This is bigger than the deadline.
closing: >-
  The VDR refactor is a dramatic, complex change to security compliance, and something that many teams are struggling to understand, implement, and maintain. It's also one of the most timely changes we could be facing. Think about it this way - at some point in the future we'll look back on the 12-hour SLA and chuckle that we used to have so much time to respond.
