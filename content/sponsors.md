The No-Cap Workshop is three days of free, hands-on, practitioner-led sessions for teams meeting FedRAMP's VDR requirements. Sponsors are why it is free for everyone, and why every session stays online afterward.

## Who is in the room

We're aiming for nationwide participation from cloud service providers across the FedRAMP community, and from the teams inside them: engineering, compliance, security, and leadership. These are the people working on this exact problem, on a deadline, and looking for what works.

## What sponsorship funds

Logistics across leads, attendees, and sponsors. Run-of-show support for the online platform. Post-processing of the recordings so they are useful long after the week is over. Private sessions are separate and are funded by the teams that request them.

## Tiers

{{< tiers >}}

Sponsors appear on a short opening card before every session, sized by tier, and in a listing on the website. Because the sessions are recorded and posted, the card goes with every recording.

## What stays independent

Sponsors do not choose leads, session content, or scope. The event is practitioner-led, and that is what makes it worth sponsoring. There is no registration, so there is no attendee list to share.

## Timeline and terms

- Founding sponsorships are held first come, first served until December 1.
- Gold, Silver, and Community sponsorships stay open until January 8.
- Logos are due January 12, so the opening cards can be produced.
- Commit by email confirmation or a one-page agreement. CriticalSec LLC invoices at commitment, net 30, and payment is due in full by January 15.
- If the event is cancelled or moved, sponsors receive a refund or a credit. Otherwise payments are non-refundable after the event starts.
