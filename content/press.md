## What It Is

**One line.** Three free, online days of practitioner-led sessions on meeting FedRAMP's VDR requirements.

**Short.** The No-CAP Workshop is three free, online days of hands-on sessions for teams meeting FedRAMP's VDR requirements. Practitioners who have done the work share what is working right now. Every session is recorded and posted for everyone.

**Medium.** FedRAMP's VDR requirements become mandatory on December 7, 2026, and offerings that miss the mark risk a Corrective Action Plan (a CAP). The No-CAP Workshop is three days of free, online, practitioner-led sessions, January 19–21, 2027, to help teams stay out of a CAP or get out of one. Day 1 covers Data Collection, Day 2 VDR Evaluation, and Day 3 Remediation and Reporting. Nobody registers. Every session is recorded and posted afterward, because at the end of the day it's our government we're all protecting.

**Why "No-CAP"?** A CAP is a Corrective Action Plan, the status a Cloud Service Provider lands in if it misses the VDR deadline. This workshop is about staying out of a CAP, or getting out if you're already in.
