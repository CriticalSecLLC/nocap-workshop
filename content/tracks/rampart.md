The Rampart Strategy enables you to immediately demonstrate progress towards a full VDR implementation by deploying a self-hosted, highly customized FedRAMP VDR management system. We'll bring the system online on the first day, either running on your laptop or deployed in a cloud environment, and populate it with the essential information from your environment that you'll need to perform a VDR evaluation. This collection phase generates your first coverage numbers, percentage values used to track your progress towards a full implementation. Coverage is gathered in a central reporting page that can be shared with both internal and external stakeholders.

Next you'll begin evaluating VDR evaluation algorithms based on the set publicly available, all of which can be tailored to your environment, or you can work to develop and implement your own approach. VDR algorithm selection(s) are run against the data you've collected to generate your first set of evaluations, the SLA targets for vulnerability remediation inside your environment. This work generates your next set of coverage numbers based on how many current findings in the environment have been triaged for VDR.

With VDR evaluations in hand, it's time to start remediation by combining the SLAs with highly enriched information about each finding to deliver actionable tickets to the people responsible for fixing the vulnerabilities. We'll discuss the options you have for remediation including rapid, AI-assisted patching, multiple ways to deal with false positives, and preventing them from showing up in the first place.

Finally, we wrap with the reporting process to tie it all together with centralized, actionable dashboards and generated reports. Reporting is tailored to views specific to each of your stakeholders: customers and AOs, internal leadership, compliance, security, and engineering. Everyone gets the data that they need to understand the true state of the system. No-CAP.
