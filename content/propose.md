Proposals for sessions and multi-day tracks are due {{< proposal-deadline >}}.

## Before You Start

- Read the [Lead Agreement](/lead-agreement/), including the evaluation criteria, the Positivity Rule, and the competition-law rules. If your proposal is approved, you agree to it.
- Have the details about your session or track ready, and look through the other [sessions and tracks](/sessions/) to get a sense of the style, scope, and information needed.
- Planning to offer private sessions? Have the name of the offering and a link for booking an introductory call ready too.

## Submit

{{< proposal-form >}}

The form is the only way to propose. Its first page asks whether you are proposing a single session or a multi-day track that runs across all three days, and takes you down the matching route. Adding co-leads and asking to be listed for [private sessions](/private-sessions/) are optional, and their pages appear when you choose them.

## What Happens Next

- Proposals stay between you, the organizer, and the [Steering Committee](/steering/), and are kept separate from sponsors. See [Privacy](/privacy/) for how your information is handled.
- The committee evaluates each proposal against the [published criteria](/lead-agreement/#evaluation-criteria), and the organizer tells you the outcome by email within five business days of the deadline. A decline comes with a short reason on request, and you may resubmit.
- A private session listing is decided in the same vote as your proposal, and depends on your public session or track being approved.
- Approved sessions are added to the [schedule](/sessions/).

## Changing Your Proposal

Use the same form. After you submit, the confirmation page and your email copy include an **Edit your response** link, and you can change your answers any time before {{< proposal-deadline >}}. Please send changes through the form so the committee always reviews your latest version.

## Questions

Questions about the process go to [leads@nocap-workshop.com](mailto:leads@nocap-workshop.com?subject=Question%20about%20proposing%20at%20No-CAP%20Workshop).
