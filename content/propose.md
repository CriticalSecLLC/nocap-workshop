## Before You Start

- Read the [Lead Agreement](/lead-agreement/), including the evaluation criteria, the Positivity Rule, and the competition-law rules. If your proposal is approved, you agree to it.
- Have the following ready: the title and a short description, the day or theme it fits (Data Collection, VDR Evaluation, or Remediation and Reporting) or an outline of a multi-day track, who will lead it with a short bio and affiliation for each lead, what attendees will do or leave with, the format and length you have in mind, the tools and data you will use, any product the session features, and any relationship you have with a sponsor.

{{< proposal-form >}}

## What Happens Next

- Proposals are seen only by the organizer and the [Steering Committee](/steering/). They are not shared with sponsors.
- The committee evaluates each proposal against the [published criteria](/lead-agreement/#evaluation-criteria), and the organizer tells you the outcome by email. A decline comes with a short reason on request, and you may resubmit.
- Approved sessions are added to the [schedule](/sessions/).

## Prefer Email?

If the form does not work for you, write to [leads@nocap-workshop.com](mailto:leads@nocap-workshop.com?subject=Leading%20a%20session%20at%20No-CAP%20Workshop) with the same information.
