## Before You Start

- Read the [Lead Agreement](/lead-agreement/), including the evaluation criteria, the Positivity Rule, and the competition-law rules. If your proposal is approved, you agree to it.
- Have the details about your session or track ready, and look through the other [sessions and tracks](/sessions/) to get a sense of the style, scope, and information needed.
- Planning to offer private sessions? Have the name of the offering and a link for booking an introductory call ready too.

## What Happens Next

- Proposals sent by the form or email stay between you, the organizer, and the [Steering Committee](/steering/), and are kept separate from sponsors. A pull request is public. See [Privacy](/privacy/) for how your information is handled.
- The committee evaluates each proposal against the [published criteria](/lead-agreement/#evaluation-criteria), and the organizer tells you the outcome within five business days of the deadline. A decline comes with a short reason on request, and you may resubmit.
- A private session listing is decided in the same vote as your proposal, and depends on your public session or track being approved.
- Approved sessions are added to the [schedule](/sessions/).

## Changing Your Proposal

Changes are welcome any time before {{< proposal-deadline >}}. If you used the form, the confirmation page and your email copy include an **Edit your response** link. If you emailed, reply on the same thread. If you opened a pull request, push more commits to the same branch. The committee always reviews your latest version.

## Questions

Questions about the process go to [leads@nocap-workshop.com](mailto:leads@nocap-workshop.com?subject=Question%20about%20proposing%20at%20No-CAP%20Workshop).
