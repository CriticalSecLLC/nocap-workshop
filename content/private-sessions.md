Private sessions are booked and hosted directly with your team. They can be arranged at any time, before or after the January Nationwide Workshop. Formats are flexible and set by each provider. Pricing is tailored to the size of your team.

Providers are leads at the workshop. The [Steering Committee](/steering/#approving-private-session-listings) decides each listing together with the provider's public session or track. A listing is a directory entry, and each provider sets their own prices and stands behind their own results. Rampart is the organizer's founding listing and appears as a placeholder while the committee assesses it like every other listing.
