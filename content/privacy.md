We collect only what we need to run the workshop. You can attend without registering or identifying yourself, and we do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## What We Collect

- **The announcement list.** If you sign up on the home page, we collect your email address. The list is run on [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy). Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
- **Proposals.** If you propose a session or track, the [proposal form](/propose/) collects your email address, name, affiliation, bio, any photo link you share, your session details, and your disclosures. The form is run on Google Forms. Your email address is used only to contact you about your proposal and is never published.
- **Attending.** There is no registration and no identification. If a session platform asks for details to join, we will say so on the schedule before the workshop.
- **Visiting this site.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, and both process visitors' IP addresses in their server logs to deliver and protect the site. See the [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) privacy statements. The site is anonymous: it sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no analytics, and shows no advertising. The home page picks a headline at random each time it loads, entirely in your browser.
- **Session attendance.** The only numbers we gather are attendance counts from the sessions themselves, reported in aggregate to sponsors after the workshop. We do not track visitors to this website.

## How We Use It

- To announce the workshop and share links to sessions.
- To review proposals, contact leads, and publish approved sessions.
- To keep the workshop running and secure.
- To produce the aggregate sponsor report, which is built from session attendance counts and contains no personal information about individual attendees.

## Who Sees It

- **Proposals** are seen only by the organizer and the [Steering Committee](/steering/), and are kept separate from sponsors. If a proposal is approved, we publish the lead's name, affiliation, bio, photo, and session description on the Leads and schedule pages.
- **Service providers** that run our tools (Buttondown, Google, GitHub, and Cloudflare) handle information on our behalf under their own terms.
- **Sponsors** receive aggregate results only.
- **Legal requests.** We may share information when the law requires it.

## Recordings

Every session is recorded and posted publicly with a transcript. If you ask a question by voice or video, or share your name in chat, you may appear in the recording. You can ask questions in a way that does not identify you, and you can [write to us](mailto:questions@nocap-workshop.com?subject=Recording%20request) afterward to ask us to review an edit.

## How Long We Keep It

We keep announcement-list emails until you unsubscribe. We keep proposals for the planning cycle and the committee's records. Approved sessions and recordings stay published so the work outlives the week.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can unsubscribe at any time, change your proposal with the **Edit your response** link, and ask us to show, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. [Write to us](mailto:questions@nocap-workshop.com?subject=Privacy%20request) and we will respond within 30 days. Some information, such as a published recording, may take longer to edit and is handled case by case.

## Security

We limit access to proposals to the organizer and the Steering Committee, and we rely on our providers' safeguards for the information they hold.

## Children

The workshop is for working professionals and is not directed at children.

## Links to Other Sites

Sessions and resources may link to other sites. Their own privacy practices apply there.

## Changes

We update this page as the workshop takes shape, and every change is tracked in its [history on GitHub](https://github.com/CriticalSecLLC/nocap-workshop/commits/main/content/privacy.md).
