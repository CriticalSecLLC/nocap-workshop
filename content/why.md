The No-CAP Workshop is driven by a small team of FedRAMP practitioners who understand just how hard it is to make any of this work. We're all focused on meeting FedRAMP's Consolidated Rules for 2026 (CR26) to implement Vulnerability Detection and Response (VDR) at the same time that the rate of celebrity vulnerabilities is going through the roof. This creates an imperative for teams to level up their game, and we wanted a way for us to collaborate on what we've learned, what's working, and how we're protecting ourselves and our customers.

## What it is

We pick a time for people working on these challenges to come together, share what they've learned, answer questions, record those sessions, and make them freely available to everyone. Instead of casting around for answers, you get them in one place directly from the people on the front lines.

- Dates: January 19–21, 2027
- Topics: Data Collection, VDR Evaluation, and Remediation and Reporting.
- Cost: Free, online, and open to anyone. No registration and no identification.

Funded by sponsors, run by practitioners, and recorded so the work outlives the week.

## What to Expect

Expect hands-on sessions led by practitioners, in a learning event rather than a certification program (although feel free to put in for continuing education credits). Vendors are highly encouraged to attend and present on what their products do to meet these requirements. If you've got a solution that tackles multiple days worth of content, present a track across them.

Nothing here is advice from FedRAMP or any agency, although if you're with either of those groups and you'd like to lead a session please [reach out](mailto:leads@nocap-workshop.com?subject=Leading%20a%20session%20at%20No-CAP%20Workshop).
