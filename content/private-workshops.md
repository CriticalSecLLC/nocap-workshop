Private workshops are working sessions hosted directly with your team. You can book one any time, before or after the January Nation-Wide Workshop, so there is no need to wait. Formats are flexible and set by each provider. Pricing is a flat fee tailored to the size of your team.

## How to book

1. Choose a provider from the table below.
2. Book an introductory call.
3. The provider tailors a private workshop to your team.
