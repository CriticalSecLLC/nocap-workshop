Call for Leads

We want sessions on what you have actually done: the tooling, the tradeoffs, the numbers. Sessions fit one of the three days (data collection, VDR Evaluation, or remediation and reporting). Sessions are recorded and posted publicly, and you may link your own webinar or write-up from the schedule. Proposals are reviewed by the Steering Committee against the published criteria, and leads agree to the Lead Agreement.

Multi-Day Product Tracks

Building a product that helps teams meet the VDR rules? Step up for a full set of talks. A product track runs across all three days, with sessions on each, and gets its own track page listing the sessions and who leads them.
