Sponsor Summary · Nationwide Workshop · Jan 19–21, 2027 · Online · Free

The Opportunity

FedRAMP has overhauled how providers triage vulnerabilities, and the new approach demands automated, sophisticated reasoning to make good decisions under tight time constraints. That's the good news. The deadlines are the hard part: the rules become mandatory December 7, 2026, and offerings under a Corrective Action Plan risk losing certification if they aren't compliant by March 7, 2027. Our response is three days of free, practitioner-led sessions on what has actually worked for assessment, mitigation, and response. Sponsors are how we make it free.

The Audience

  • Cloud service providers across the FedRAMP community, and the engineering, compliance, security, and leadership teams
  • Auditors, advisors, and vendors who serve them
  • Bottom Line: These are the people working on this exact problem, at scale, on a deadline.
